## 🎯 BEST PRACTICES FOR POSTING

### ✅ DO:
1. **Post during peak hours** (9-10 AM, 12 PM, 5-6 PM, 8 PM)
2. **Use visual assets** - Include charts, graphs, screenshots
3. **Ask questions** - Encourage comments ("Drop your insights below!")
4. **Use relevant hashtags** - 15-25 hashtags per post
5. **Share genuine learning** - People connect with authenticity
6. **Engage comments** - Reply within 1 hour for first comments
7. **Update frequently** - Post 3-4x per week for consistency
8. **Cross-platform** - Share on Twitter, Medium, Portfolio

### ❌ DON'T:
1. Over-promote (avoid sales language)
2. Make claims without evidence
3. Ignore comments/messages
4. Copy-paste generic content
5. Post at random times
6. Forget CTAs (Calls-to-Action)
7. Shy away from technical details
8. Be modest about achievements
